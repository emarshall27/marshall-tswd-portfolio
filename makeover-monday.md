## Original Visualization: 
### https://makeovermonday.vercel.app/dataset/what-are-the-world-s-deadliest-animals 

## Selection Choice: 
### I chose this visualization because I thought the topic was interesting and somewhat surprising. Most people probably assume that larger or more dangerous-looking animals, like sharks, lions, or crocodiles, are responsible for the most human deaths. However, the data shows that much smaller animals, particularly mosquitoes, are responsible for significantly more deaths each year. I also thought this visualization would be interesting to redesign because it presents a lot of information that could be made easier to understand and compare. I want to explore how I can make the data more visually engaging while still clearly communicating the differences between animals and the number of deaths associated with them. 

## Critique Findings and Insights: 
### The biggest thing that stood out to me was how mosquitoes account for such a large number of human deaths compared to animals that are generally considered more dangerous, like sharks and lions. I think the visualization does a good job of highlighting these differences and making the topic interesting. However, I think the amount of information could be organized more clearly, and some of the comparisons are difficult to interpret at a glance. I would also like to see a stronger visual hierarchy that makes the most important information stand out without requiring the viewer to spend too much time figuring out what they are looking at.

## Redesign Process: 
### For my redesign, I want to focus on making the data easier to compare and understand at a glance while also making it more visually engaging. A few things I would like to try are: (1) Use a horizontal bar chart to make it easier to compare the number of human deaths associated with each animal. (2) Highlight the most deadly animals with a different color or visual emphasis so the biggest differences stand out immediately. (3) Organize the animals by number of deaths, from highest to lowest, rather than making the viewer search for the comparisons themselves. (4) Include clearer labels and context so viewers can quickly understand what the numbers represent. I'm excited to experiment with different ways of presenting the data and see if I can make the visualization simpler, more interesting, and easier to interpret without losing any of the important information.

## Explanation of Redesign: 
### My redesigned visualization shows the estimated number of human deaths caused by different animals each year, ranked from highest to lowest. I changed the original design into a horizontal bar chart to make the numbers easier to compare and the rankings more obvious. I also simplified the overall layout, used color to highlight the two categories with the highest death tolls, and made the numerical labels easier to see. I added a clearer title and subtitle so viewers can immediately understand what the data represents. Overall, my goal was to make the visualization cleaner, easier to read, and more effective at highlighting how much of a difference there is between the animals.
